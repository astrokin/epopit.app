Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to ePopIt, a mobile application developed by Remione LTD ("we," "our," "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your Personal Data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our app and how the law protects your privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information:

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, and other contact details when you register for an account or contact us for support.

Usage Data:

We collect information on how you use our app, including your interactions, preferences, and device information.

Log Data:

In the event of an error in the app, we collect data such as your device Internet Protocol (IP) address, device name, operating system version, and other relevant statistics.

Game Data (Stored Locally and in iCloud):

Information relating to your progress and achievements in the game is stored locally on your device and in Apple iCloud. This data ensures you can continue your game where you left off. This data is not accessible to us.

Cookies:

Our app may use third-party services that collect information using cookies. You have the option to accept or refuse these cookies.

3. Use of Third-Party Services

We use third-party services, such as: Google Play Services, Firebase Analytics, Ironsource, Adapty. Which may collect and use your personal data. These services help us analyze how our app is used and improve the overall user experience.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to: Provide, operate, and maintain our app. Improve, personalize, and expand our services. Communicate with you for updates, promotions, and customer support. Monitor and analyze usage and trends. Show personalized advertisements relevant to you. Ensure security and prevent fraud.

5. Underage Players

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13, in compliance with applicable laws, including GDPR and CCPA. If you or the adult who created your Game Center ID set your age as underage, no personal data will be collected. To change this, speak with the adult who controls your Game Center ID.

6. Data Retention

Game Data:

Your game data is stored in Apple’s iCloud system as long as you have an active game account. This data ensures you can continue playing the game where you last stopped and is used solely for this purpose.

Other Data:

Your data will be retained as long as your account is active. If you request the removal of personal data, we will process the request in compliance with legal obligations.

7. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Disclosures to Third Parties

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties, except as necessary to operate the app, comply with the law, or protect our legal rights.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to: Request access to your personal data. Rectify incorrect personal data. Erase your personal data under certain circumstances. Restrict or object to the processing of your personal data. Receive your data so that you can use it elsewhere (data portability). To exercise these rights, contact us at support@epopit.app. We will respond to your requests within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 4 weeks. If your data is processed by Apple, you may need to contact Apple directly to exercise these rights.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
